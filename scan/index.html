Running it for the first time

Windows
  1. Unzip the download.
  2. Open FactsScan.exe. No administrator rights are needed.
  3. The program is new and not yet signed, so Windows SmartScreen may ask you to confirm. Choose More info, then Run anyway.
Mac
  1. Unzip the download.
  2. Right-click FACTS Scan, choose Open, then Open again. This is needed once, because the program is not from the App Store.
  3. When macOS asks whether FACTS Scan may find devices on your local network, choose Allow. The scan cannot work without it.

Your browser then shows the scan window. Tick the networks to look at, start the scan, and save the report.

What it does, and does not do

  • It only looks. It never changes, controls or writes to a machine.
  • It scans private address ranges only, and only the ones you tick.
  • Nothing is sent anywhere. The report is saved on your own computer.
  • It is gentle on the network, and you can stop it at any time.
  • A one-page note for your IT team is included in the download.
